National Institutes of Health · post-bac · about 6h to apply

Deadline months are typical windows, not scraped dates. Confirm on the linked page two weeks before a window opens, and correct config/programs.yaml if it has moved.
Sponsorship reads are inferred from posting language and employer type, not a verified database. Treat explicit as reliable and everything else as a question to ask.
Cap exempt means a university, affiliated nonprofit or nonprofit research institute, which can file an H-1B outside the annual lottery.
Permit means a role outside the US: no F-1 question, but a local work permit is still needed and the posting says nothing about supporting one.
Roles asking for more than two years of experience, a doctorate, or bench work in a named professor's university laboratory are removed. The employer stays in the registry either way, so it is still watched for roles you could actually get.
Posting dates come from the employer's own system where possible. A tilde means the date came from a feed and is roughly right; no date published means the board does not publish one, and those never rank as fresh. Anything the employer dated more than 75 days ago is dropped, and roles requiring citizenship, permanent residence or a clearance are removed rather than shown.
